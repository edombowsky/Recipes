I had no idea a Japanese curry existed until I started researching curries. It is so nostalgic, yet foreign at the same time. Reminds me of a stew my grandma would make but then it is reinforced with Asian flavours like curry powder and dashi. If you like curry, you must try this one out.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb chicken thighs, diced 1 ½" cubes
- [ ] 1 ¼ teaspoons kosher salt
- [ ] 8 oz Yukon gold potatoes, large diced
- [ ] 1 ½ cups carrots, large diced
- [ ] 1 ½ cup shiitake mushrooms, quartered
- [ ] 1 cup yellow onion, small diced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- [ ] 1 tablespoon grated garlic
- [ ] 3 tablespoon Japanese curry powder, sub Indian curry powder
- [ ] 2 teaspoon garam masala
- [ ] 8 tablespoon butter, 1 stick
- [ ] ½ cup flour
- [ ] 4 cups chicken stock
- [ ] 2 tablespoons instant dashi powder, or bouillon
- [ ] 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 3 tablespoons honey
- [ ] high smoke point oil

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, salt the chicken thighs and let them sit for at least 10 minutes.
2. Heat your Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add in 2 tablespoons of oil followed by the chicken thighs. Sear the chicken thighs for 5 minutes, until some colour has formed.
3. Add in the onions and mushrooms and cook until the onions are soft, translucent and just starting to caramelize, roughly 5 minutes. Deglaze with a little bit of water if needed to scrape up the brown bits.
4. Stir in garlic and ginger and cook for 1–2 minutes.
5. Stir in the curry powder and garam masala. Allow the spices to toast for 1 minute and then add in the butter.
6. Allow the butter to melt fully and then stir in the flour. Cook for one minute and then add in the stock and dashi powder. Bring the pot to a simmer.
7. Add in the potatoes, carrots, soy, Worcestershire, and honey.
8. Give everything a nice stir and the cover and simmer on medium-low heat for 15-20 minutes stirring occasionally. When the vegetables are tender, the soup is ready. Adjust seasoning or consistency if needed with salt and stock. Serve with Japanese rice.
