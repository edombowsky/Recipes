## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 carrots
- [ ] 1 yellow onion
- [ ] 4 celery stalks
- [ ] 1 whole bunch of spinach
- [ ] 5 chicken thighs (bone in and skinless)
- [ ] 3 cloves of garlic
- [ ] 2.5 tablespoons of tomato paste
- [ ] 4 cups of chicken stock
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Half cup of water
- [ ] Dried Thyme
- [ ] Bayleaf
- [ ] 1 cup heavy cream
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] Parmesan cheese shredded (optional)
- [ ] These seasonings below are no measurements. Use as much or little as you want.
	- [ ] Italian seasoning
	- [ ] Onion powder
	- [ ] Garlic powder
	- [ ] Lemon Pepper seasoning (salt free)
	- [ ] Paprika
	- [ ] Salt
	- [ ] Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Season chicken thighs with all the seasonings immediately above.
2. Brown chicken in Dutch oven with a tbsp of butter for 3–4 minutes per side on medium heat.
3. Pull chicken when browned on each side to develop colour and a crust and then set aside.
4. Take the diced onions, celery stalks and carrots and toss em in with a tbsp of butter.
5. Sprinkle a pinch of salt. Tiny little guy.
6. Cook until veggies start to look soft, about 6–7 minutes on medium heat.
7. Mix in diced garlic + tomato paste and cook for about 3 minutes.
8. 2.5 tablespoons of flour and mix it into the veggies. This will help with the thickness of the soup. Cook 2 minutes.
9. Take chicken stalk and gradually mix it in with a wooden spoon. Stir and pour, stir, and pour, stir, and pour. Do that a few times until it's all combined and you've scraped the bottom of the pot and bring to a simmer.
10. Put browned chicken, dried thyme and 2 bay leafs in and mix. Put the lid on and let it cook for 20 minutes.
11. After 20 minutes, take the chicken out of the liquid and place into a bowl. LET THE CHICKEN REST. My dumb*** tried pulling this apart with my bare hands 2 minutes after I took it out. It's hot. Let it chill for a few means and then shred it.
12. Put the shredded chicken back into the pot.
13. Stir in heavy cream and add additional seasonings to the broth now. Salt, pepper, paprika etc. and taste to your liking. I like black pepper and paprika so I was heavy with those. At this point I thought just the broth by itself was looking too thick so I added half a cup of water. You could make this soupier if you wanted, just add more water here.
14. Cover and go make your dumplings, takes about 5-8 minutes
15. Combine 1 cup all‑purpose flour, 1 teaspoon baking powder, green onion, 0.5 cup milk, black pepper and salt then mix until just combined. This is straight from the recipe except I used green onion instead of chives. I would have also added some chicken broth or bouillon for next time to the mix to give it some more flavour. They were good. But could be more with additional seasonings. Maybe a different flour? I used all purpose.
16. Make dumplings - Take the batter and make little meatballs with your hands and then lightly drop them into the soup. Then cover the pot and let them steam for 10 minutes.
17. Put spinach into the pot and cook for 3 minutes (this was harder to too do with the size of the dumplings I made. I had to fit the spinach in around the dumplings. It was fine but if someone with more culinary experience has a better time to put em in let me know).
18. Add shredded Parmesan if you'd like. Again the photo above has none.
19. Eat the f**k out of it. It was so good. 10/10 would make again.
