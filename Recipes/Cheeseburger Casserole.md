People make casseroles for a reason, but they’re typically a little bland and mushy. My cheeseburger casserole takes what you know about the classic casserole and makes it worthy of any party, potluck, or weeknight dinner.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 12 oz large elbow macaroni
- [ ] 1 lb ground beef, 90/10
- [ ] 1 cup diced onion
- [ ] 4 teaspoons minced garlic
- [ ] 3 cups baby bella mushrooms, sliced
- [ ] 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- [ ] ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 ¼ cup beef stock
- [ ] 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 2 ½ tablespoon Better Than Bouillon beef base, or 25g powdered beef bouillon
- [ ] 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1 cup heavy cream
- [ ] ⅓ cup sour cream, add off heat
- [ ] 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- [ ] ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] fried onions, crumbled, store-bought

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425 ℉.
2. Bring 4 quarts of water to a boil. Salt well and then cook macaroni until al dente. Strain off the noodles, coat them with olive oil, and set aside.
3. In a Dutch oven over medium-high, cook ground beef with a pinch of salt, breaking it down finely as it cooks. When the beef is no longer pink, add onion, garlic, and another pinch of salt. Cook, scraping up any ground beef fond, until onions are soft (5 minutes).
4. Add mushrooms and a pinch of salt and cook until softened and lightly brown (5–8 minutes). Add a bit of water to deglaze as mushrooms cook.
5. Once mushrooms have begun to brown, stir in tomato paste and flour. Cook briefly, then add beef stock, Worcestershire, beef bouillon, Dijon, and heavy cream. Simmer for 2–3 minutes.
6. Off heat, stir in sour cream. The sauce should be thick and creamy.
7. Fold pasta with sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Adjust with a little water if needed.
8. Transfer to a baking dish. Top with crumbled fried onions and more Parmesan.
9. Bake at 425°F/220C for 20–25 minutes until golden and bubbly.
