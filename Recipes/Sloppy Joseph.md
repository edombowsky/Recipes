Sloppy Joe tucked his shirt in and wants to meet the parents. This recipe has more developed flavours and is a little less sloppy than the Joe you grew up eating.

## Ingredients

- [ ] lb 80/20 ground beef, .5kg
- [ ] 1 g or 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- [ ] 100 g or 1 large or 2 small poblano peppers small diced
- [ ] 150 g or 1 large yellow or white onion, small diced
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] 5 g or 1 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 5 g or 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
- [ ] 5 g or 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- [ ] 2 g or 1 teaspoon black pepper
- [ ] 5 g or 1 3/4 teaspoons paprika
- [ ] Pinch of Chile flakes
- [ ] 10 g or 1 1/2 tablespoons flour, all purpose or gluten free blend
- [ ] 50 g or 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- [ ] 50 g or 3 tablespoons ketchup
- [ ] 15 g or 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
- [ ] 15 g or 2 1/2–3 teaspoons brown sugar
- [ ] 50 g or 3 1/2 tablespoons Worcestershire
- [ ] 20 g or 1 1/2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- [ ] 10 g or 1 ½ teaspoons better than bouillon beef paste
- [ ] 350 g or 1 1/2 cup stock, chicken or beef

## Instructions

1. Add beef to a bowl with baking soda and mix thoroughly to incorporate.
2. Preheat a large nonstick pan over medium high then add a squeeze (tablespoon or so) of olive oil followed by diced onions, poblanos, and a pinch of salt. Saute for 3–4 minutes, stirring often.
3. When veg has softened a bit and has started to brown, add in beef/baking soda mix, using a meat musher or wooden spoon to break up meat into small pieces.
4. When beef is broken down well and is beginning to brown, add salt (5g), onion powder, garlic powder, pepper, paprika, and chilli flake. Stir to combine and cook for about 30 seconds.
5. Add flour and stir to combine and cook for a few seconds.
6. Add tomato products, mustard, brown sugar, Worcestershire, vinegar, bouillon, and stock. Stir to combine. Bring to a simmer then reduce heat to medium low to reduce for about 10–12 minutes, continuing to stir and break up meat as sauce reduces.
7. Taste for seasoning and add salt if needed.
8. Serve on toasted brioche bun.
